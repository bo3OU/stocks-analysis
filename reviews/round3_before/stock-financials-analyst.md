## Start and cutoff
1. Read the contract, `ROOT/skills/finviz-elite-export/SKILL.md`, `ROOT/skills/sec-edgar-access/SKILL.md`, then `RESEARCH_DIR/INVENTORY.md`, `manifest.json`, `identity.json` and `filings_index.csv`. The contract overrides skill examples requesting direct SEC/Finviz calls or `.env` access. Resolve manifest `path` against `ROOT/research/`. Check issuer, accounting basis, currency, share class, fiscal dates, files and caps. The filings reader's `findings.md` and `claims.jsonl` are required handoffs; if missing, reply `BLOCKED: ...`. If primary accounts are unavailable, identify blocked tables and complete supported work.
2. Read the filings reader's claims and locations for obligations, restricted cash, contract liabilities, shares, instruments and later events. Verify material amounts and ambiguous definitions in targeted filing slices. Use `grep -n`, narrow `sed -n` slices and original tables when conversion is doubtful; never print whole filings or companyfacts.
3. Select filings and releases available by `AS_OF` using `filings_index.csv`, acceptance times and release metadata. Exclude same-day sources with unknown publication time at an intraday cutoff. For companyfacts use only eligible accessions and applicable amendments/restatements; `filed` alone cannot establish intraday eligibility. Explain excluded later restatements. An eligible earnings release preceding the periodic filing supports its disclosed unaudited figures, with missing notes flagged. Finviz statements are live snapshots: use the manifest's `retrieved_at` for an `ok`/`cached` entry only if its provenance establishes retrieval by `AS_OF`; `cached` alone proves nothing. If first availability is uncertain, omit the Finviz comparison and log why. Never reconstruct an earlier snapshot. Reuse cache; fetch material missing SEC/Finviz sources through `agent_fetch.py`, or ask the orchestrator for a wider cache.

## Work
4. Save in `work/`: Python scripts, extracted input rows with accession/source, period, unit and location, `financial_history.csv`, `reconciliation.csv` and `calc_appendix.md` (definitions, formulas, assumptions, run command). Run scripts with `ROOT/.venv/bin/python -I`. Point `findings.md` to tables for valuation. FACT figures need opened sources; CALC figures need code and input claim IDs. Never invent missing values.
5. Build 5–10 comparable annual years, eight quarters and TTM where primary records support them; check the index beyond the pipeline's three annual/eight interim cache cap and obtain material missing reports or state the gap. Use filing period ends; Finviz normalized dates are labels. Each `financial_history.csv` row gives period type/start/end, metric/value, accounting basis, currency/unit, source, claim ID and status. Include revenue, gross/operating profit and margins, net income, CFO, capex, defined FCF, cash, debt, and distinct basic/diluted weighted and dated outstanding shares where disclosed. Explain spin-off/predecessor, accounting and currency breaks; never insert parent accounts. Verify XBRL tag, unit, accession, duration/instant, quarter/YTD, comparatives, restatement and continuing-operation scope against the filing. Derive standalone quarterly cash flows from consecutive same-year YTD figures, and Q4 from annual less nine-month YTD, only when definitions and scope match; label CALC. Never treat YTD as standalone. Build TTM from four nonoverlapping quarters or fiscal year plus current YTD less corresponding prior YTD; missing components mean not obtained. Reconcile eligible Finviz TTM only after period, currency and definition checks.
6. In `work/reconciliation.csv`, compare available primary filing figures with XBRL and eligible Finviz for latest annual and interim revenue, gross/operating profit, net income, CFO, capex, cash, debt and shares. Record values, periods, units, currency, source locations and status: match / explained definition difference / unresolved / unavailable. Do not force three-way comparisons or assign causes without evidence. Filing and notes govern reported-account definitions; disclose unresolved conflicts. If currencies differ, do not subtract or assert a Finviz line's FX method without verification.
7. Explain revenue changes by disclosed volume, price, mix, acquisitions, FX and accounting components; mark undisclosed components UNKNOWN. Compare GAAP/IFRS with management-adjusted CLAIMs, definitions and recurring exclusions. Assess SBC, impairments, restructuring, taxes, CFO/net income, FCF/net income, working capital and capitalized costs where material. Compute DSO/DIO/DPO, accruals and ROIC only with stated numerator, average balances, denominator, period and sector fit; mark negative/near-zero denominators N/M with reason. Any investigative threshold is your flag, never an accounting rule or misconduct finding.
8. Define FCF as CFO less capex; show acquisitions, finance leases and capitalized software separately where disclosed. Avoid double-counting SBC cash and dilution. From filings reader claims and verified notes assess unrestricted cash, debt/maturities, interest, leases, covenants, guarantees, contingent obligations and conditional funding. Bridge balance-sheet dates to `AS_OF` for material disclosed financings or cash changes; unavailable current balance is UNKNOWN. Shelf/ATM authorization is not cash. For cash-consuming companies, script base and stress runway scenarios with justified cash flows and milestone dates; label assumptions. Distinguish converts, warrants, options, preferred, earnouts and announced financing from issued shares; use consistent split/ADR basis. Adapt metrics for banks, insurers, REITs and pre-revenue firms and explain inapplicable industrial ratios.
