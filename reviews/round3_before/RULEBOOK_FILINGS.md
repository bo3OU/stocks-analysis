# Rulebook for reading filings (used by `stock-filings-reader`)

Rules produce **review flags**, never findings of misconduct or legal conclusions. Every flag records rule ID, person/class, accession, transaction/event date, filing date, inputs, calculation, source location and false-positive risks. If status, exemption or a required date is unknown, mark the check UNKNOWN rather than a suspected violation. Verify the law applicable on the transaction date and jurisdiction before using a flag.

The Status column says what was actually opened on 2026-11-09 and what was not; it does not establish what was knowable at an earlier AS_OF. Guidance pages explain general rules, not the facts of an individual filing. For historical research verify the then-effective rule and dated exemptions; reopen the cited rule/guidance and transaction footnotes for a material flag. `derived/` rows are screening inputs; the original filing controls.

| S16-A | Most reportable Form 4 transactions are due within two business days | `insider_filings.csv`: `date`, `filed`, `form`, `code`, `relationship` | Screen original Form 4 dates with the applicable US business-day/holiday calendar; inspect amendments and exceptions before flagging a late *candidate* | **Verified 2026-11-09**: SEC page [officers-directors-10-shareholders](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/going-public/officers-directors-10-shareholders): most transactions reported 'within two business days' | Form 5 eligibility, deemed execution, holidays, filer status and amended records |
| S16-B | Section 16(b) concerns certain nonexempt opposite-way transactions within six months | `owner_ciks`, `code`, `date`, `price`, `shares`, `security`, `table` | Domestic issuers only: list same-owner, same-security, nonderivative P/S pairs as *review candidates*; show dates, prices and shares. Do not compute recoverable profit or call a pair a violation | **Verified (principle) 2026-11-09**: same SEC page: profits from a purchase and sale within six months; the company recovers. Matching method and exemptions **not verified** | Exemptions, beneficial ownership, security equivalence, insider status, amendments |
| S16-C | From 2026-03-18, specified foreign private issuer directors/officers became subject to Section 16(a) reporting; 10% holders solely as such excluded; Section 16(b) remains inapplicable | `identity.json` `filer_type`; `filed`, `relationship` | For an FPI, skip S16-B; check whether Section 12 registration and any SEC jurisdiction exemption applies before screening Form 4 timeliness. Pre-effective-date silence is not a gap | **Verified 2026-11-09**: SEC [HFIA guidance page](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-corporation-finance/holding-foreign-insiders-accountable-act-section-16a-reporting-requirements) (effective March 18, 2026 for directors and officers of foreign private issuers; exemptive orders for listed jurisdictions) and final rule [Release 34-104903](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2026/34-104903.pdf) (directors and officers, not 10% holders; the Act did not amend §16(b)). Our TSM data: first Form 4 filed 2026-03-18 | FPI status date, registration, exempt jurisdictions, filing transitions |
| R10B5-A | Rule 10b5-1 affirmative-defense cooling-off: for directors/officers, later of 90 days after adoption/modification or two business days after results in the relevant 10-Q/10-K, with 120-day cap; other persons excluding issuer: 30 days | `rule_10b5_1`, `footnotes`, `date`, `relationship`; periodic report filing/acceptance from `filings_index.csv` | Screen only when plan date, role, applicable report and transaction are verified in originals. Calculate both branches and cap; a result inside the window is a *review candidate*, not proof of unlawful trading. Otherwise UNKNOWN | **Verified 2026-11-09**: SEC [fact sheet for release 33-11138](https://www.sec.gov/files/33-11138-fact-sheet.pdf): later of 90 days or two business days after results for the quarter, capped at 120 days (directors and officers); 30 days for others except issuers. A condition of the affirmative defense | Adoption versus material modification, transitions, sale exemption, checkbox is filing-level |
| R10B5-B | Forms 4/5 include a plan checkbox and adoption date; Item 408 requires specified periodic issuer disclosures | `rule_10b5_1`, footnotes; 10-Q/10-K Part II Other Information | Compare same person, plan and reporting quarter; flag a documented contradiction, not a missing mention in an unrelated period | **Verified 2026-11-09**: SEC [compliance guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/insider-trading-arrangements-and-related-disclosures): Form 4/5 checkbox and adoption date (reports filed on or after 2023-04-01); Item 408 quarterly disclosure | Filing-level checkbox, different plans/persons, disclosure scope and effective dates |
| F144-A | Form 144 is a proposed-sale notice for an applicable affiliate sale exceeding 5,000 shares or $50,000 in any three-month period; it is not an execution record | `form144_notices.csv`: `person`, `units_to_sell`, `aggregate_market_value`, `approx_sale_date`, `plan_adoption_date`, `past_3m_sales`; Form 4 sales | List notices and any same-person Form 4 sale in searched coverage. If unmatched, state only `no matching record found` with dates, identity and coverage; the arbitrary 30-day window is not a compliance test | **Verified 2026-11-09**: SEC [Rule 144 page](https://www.sec.gov/reports/rule-144-selling-restricted-control-securities) (more than 5,000 shares or $50,000 in any three months; page last updated 2013). Filing timing relative to the sale **not verified** | Sale may not occur; attribution/entity differences; Form 4 may not apply |
| S13-A | Schedule 13D/G reports specified beneficial ownership above 5%, subject to category-specific timing | `ownership_13dg.csv`: `reporting_person`, `percent_of_class`, `aggregate_shares`, `event_date`, `filed`, `amendment_no`, `form` | List dated disclosed holdings and material changes; do not assess timeliness without verifying filer category, trigger and applicable rule. State parser coverage, including legacy filings | **Unverified**: 13D initial within five business days and amendments within two business days, XML required from 2024-12-18 come from law-firm summaries of release 33-11253; 13G timing not verified. Reopen the SEC rule text before any timeliness flag | `event_date` may not be crossing date; amendments and shared ownership; pre-XML files omitted |
| E8K-A | 8-K item codes classify company-reported events; 6-Ks have no equivalent item codes | `filings_index.csv` `items`, `filingDate`, `acceptanceDateTime`; filing and exhibits | Build a dated queue from the index. Open relevant 8-K and exhibits, especially 1.01, 2.03, 3.02, 5.02; give 4.01 and 4.02 separate entries. Confirm item meaning in the original form before describing it; never infer economic size from a code | **Unverified**: item meanings from the Form 8-K instructions via a second-opinion review; confirm in [form8-k.pdf](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) before quoting | An exhibit may be a release, not a call transcript; index caps or foreign form differences |
| TXT-A | Going-concern and internal-control disclosure review | `text/` annual/interim filing passages | Search for "substantial doubt", "going concern", "material weakness", "not effective"; open the full surrounding passage, record period, speaker and whether the concern is stated as alleviated | Source is the original filing; disclosure location depends on form | Negations, boilerplate and foreign-form differences |

Not rules, so never present them as such: any threshold you or another agent chooses (for example receivables growth above revenue growth by 20 points) is an investigative threshold and must be labelled as yours.
