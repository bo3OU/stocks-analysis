# Rulebook for reading filings (used by `stock-filings-reader`)

Rules produce **review flags**, never findings of misconduct. Every flag records: rule ID, person or class, accession, transaction/event date, filing date, the exact inputs and calculation, the source location, and the false-positive risks. If a required date or exemption is unknown, do not flag; log it as UNKNOWN. Each rule shows its verification status.

Verified = I opened the cited SEC text on 2026-11-09. Unverified = taken from a secondary source (a second-opinion review or law-firm summaries); confirm against the cited rule text before stating it as fact in a report.

| ID | Rule | Inputs (columns in `derived/`) | Check | Status and source | False-positive risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S16-A | Section 16 insiders (directors, officers, >10% holders) report most transactions on Form 4 within two business days | `insider_filings.csv`: `date` (transaction), `filed`, `form`, `code` | flag when `filed` is more than 2 business days after `date`; exclude amendments (`4/A`) from the clock; report count and examples | **Verified**: SEC page "Officers, Directors and 10% Shareholders" (sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/going-public/officers-directors-10-shareholders): "within two business days" for most transactions | some transactions have different reporting timing (e.g., reportable on Form 5), holidays, deemed execution dates |
| S16-B | Short-swing profit (§16(b)): profit from a purchase and sale (or sale and purchase) by an insider within six months is recoverable by the company | `insider_filings.csv`: `owner_ciks`, `code` (P purchase, S sale), `date`, `price`, `shares`, `security`, `table=non_derivative_transaction` | for **domestic issuers only**, list candidate pairs: same owner, same security, opposite open-market direction (`P`/`S`), dates within six months; show dates, prices, shares; label "candidate". Do NOT compute recoverable profit and never call a pair a violation | **Verified (principle)**: same SEC page (six-month period; the company recovers). Matching method and exemptions (Rule 16b-3, grants, indirect holdings) **not verified here** | exempt transactions, indirect ownership, status changes; raw P/S matching is insufficient |
| S16-C | Foreign private issuers: officers and directors began filing Forms 3/4/5 from 2026-03-18 (HFIA); 10% holders alone excluded; §16(b) does not apply | `identity.json` `filer_type`; first `filed` date in `insider_filings.csv` | if `filer_type` starts with "foreign", skip S16-B and note the regime | **Partly verified**: TSM's first Form 4 in our data is filed 2026-03-18 (FACT from data). The legal statement comes from a second-opinion review citing SEC HFIA guidance (**unverified**) | exemptions by jurisdiction |
| R10B5-A | Rule 10b5-1 plan cooling-off: directors and officers may not trade under a new or modified plan until the later of (1) 90 days after adoption/modification or (2) two business days after the issuer's periodic report with results for the quarter in which the plan was adopted/modified, capped at 120 days; others (not the issuer, directors or officers) 30 days | `insider_filings.csv`: `rule_10b5_1` (filing-level checkbox), `footnotes` (plan adoption date appears in footnote text, e.g. "adopted ... on March 4, 2026"), `date`, `relationship`; `earnings_events.csv` for results dates | extract the adoption date from footnotes by exact text; compute days from adoption to the sale date; flag if fewer than 90 days for a director/officer (the two-business-day alternative needs the periodic-report date: use `filings_index.csv` 10-Q/10-K filing dates, otherwise mark UNKNOWN); flag fewer than 30 days for others | **Verified**: SEC fact sheet for release 33-11138 (sec.gov/files/33-11138-fact-sheet.pdf) | the filing may omit a modification date; the trade may not be the first under the plan; the checkbox is at filing level, not per transaction |
| R10B5-B | Forms 4/5 carry a checkbox for trades under a 10b5-1 plan and require the plan adoption date; Item 408 requires quarterly disclosure of adoption/termination of plans by officers and directors in the 10-Q/10-K | `rule_10b5_1`, footnotes; 10-Q/10-K Part II "Other Information" text | compare Form 4 adoption dates with the issuer's quarterly disclosure; flag conflicts or a plan that appears in one but not the other | **Verified**: SEC compliance guide "Insider Trading Arrangements and Related Disclosures" (checkbox, adoption date, Item 408 quarterly disclosure; Form 4/5 changes for reports filed on or after 2023-04-01) | different persons or arrangements; reporting-quarter timing |
| F144-A | Form 144 is a notice of proposed sale by affiliates: required when the sale is more than 5,000 shares or more than $50,000 in any three-month period | `form144_notices.csv`: `person`, `units_to_sell`, `aggregate_market_value`, `approx_sale_date`, `plan_adoption_date`, `past_3m_sales`; `insider_filings.csv` sales | list notices; for each, look for a Form 4 sale (`code` S) by the same person within 30 days after `approx_sale_date`; flag notices with no match for review | **Verified**: SEC page "Rule 144" (thresholds; page last updated 2013). Timing relative to the sale **not verified here** | a notice is an intention, not an executed sale; the Form 4 may be filed under another entity |
| S13-A | Schedule 13D/G: holders above 5% report beneficial ownership; structured XML required from 2024-12-18 | `ownership_13dg.csv`: `reporting_person`, `percent_of_class`, `aggregate_shares`, `event_date`, `filed`, `amendment_no`, `form` | list holders and their percent over time; flag large changes between amendments; do not judge timeliness unless the trigger date is established | Initial 13D within five business days and 13D amendments within two business days: **unverified** (law-firm summaries of release 33-11253); 13G timing now follows quarter-end cycles: **not verified** | `event_date` is not necessarily the threshold-crossing date; legacy `SC 13D/G` filings before the XML date are not parsed (see INVENTORY.md) |
| E8K-A | 8-K item codes identify reported events: 1.01 entry into a material definitive agreement, 1.02 termination, 1.05 material cybersecurity incident, 2.01 acquisition/disposition completed, 2.02 results of operations, 2.03 creation of a direct financial obligation, 2.04 triggering events accelerating an obligation, 2.05 exit costs, 2.06 material impairments, 3.01 delisting notice, 3.02 unregistered equity sales, 3.03 material change to security holder rights, 4.01 change of certifying accountant, 4.02 non-reliance on previously issued financial statements, 5.01 change in control, 5.02 departure or appointment of directors/officers, 5.03 charter or bylaw amendments, 5.07 shareholder vote, 7.01 Regulation FD, 8.01 other events, 9.01 exhibits | `filings_index.csv` `items`, `filingDate`, `acceptanceDateTime`; exhibits in `text/` | build a dated event queue; always open and summarize exhibits for 1.01, 2.03, 3.02, 5.02; every 4.01 and 4.02 gets its own entry linked to later amended filings | **Unverified here** (item list from the Form 8-K instructions via a second-opinion review; widely known, confirm against sec.gov/forms/form8-k.pdf before quoting) | an item code is a reported event, not its economic size; foreign private issuers' 6-Ks have no item codes |
| TXT-A | Going concern and internal-control language | `text/` 10-K and 10-Q files | search for "substantial doubt", "going concern", "material weakness", "not effective"; record the exact passage, period and whether doubt is stated as alleviated | Standard disclosure locations (10-K Items 8 and 9A; 10-Q Part I Items 1 and 4): **unverified here** | boilerplate, negations ("no material weakness"); read the passage in context |

Not rules, so never present them as such: any threshold you or another agent chooses (for example receivables growth above revenue growth by 20 points) is an investigative threshold and must be labelled as yours.
