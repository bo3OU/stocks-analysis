3. **Extract notes with filing-level inline XBRL first, then bounded text parsing, then an LLM for ambiguity.** The SEC’s Company Facts API includes non-custom, whole-entity facts; it cannot by itself supply custom tags and dimensional note tables. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces). Build a script that downloads the filing’s inline-XBRL package and extension taxonomy; records each fact’s concept, context dates, dimensions, unit, scale/sign, footnote, accession, and source element; then maps known debt-maturity, lease, contract-liability, segment/geography, and concentration concepts to normalized tables. Reconcile dimensional members and maturity buckets to reported totals, preserve unmapped custom concepts, and reject incompatible periods or units. Use section-aware text/table parsing for untagged obligations and named customers, retaining the exact passage and table coordinates. The LLM should interpret unusual contract terms, explain changes and missing disclosure, and verify low-confidence mappings against the rendered note. It should never invent a missing schedule or silently fill an untagged cell. Inline XBRL’s dimensions and contexts are part of the filed data. [SEC XBRL guide](https://www.sec.gov/files/edgar/filer-information/specifications/xbrl-guide-2026-01-16.pdf). The financial-statement-note locations are **Form 10-K Item 8 and Form 10-Q Part I Item 1**.

5. **Architecture.** The largest risks are point-in-time contamination, silently incomplete coverage, and a plausible narrative assembled from unverified derived figures. The one change I would make now is a **cutoff-aware evidence ledger and release gate**: every derived row and claim links to an immutable source, accession or publication time, exact location, period, unit, transformation, and coverage status; the report cannot mark a material family or calculation complete until those fields and the primary reconciliation pass. This makes later agents cheaper because they receive validated, compact facts and explicit gaps instead of rereading a large undifferentiated cache. No files were modified.
