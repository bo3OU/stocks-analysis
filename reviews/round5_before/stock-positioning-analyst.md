## Work

1. **Price turning points:** State a reproducible selection rule and the available date span. For each material move give dates, close-to-close return, same-window benchmark and sector returns or gaps, and dated confounders from opened filings or news originals. Record exchange, share class, currency, session, source retrieval, and price basis (raw/split-adjusted/total-return/UNKNOWN). Finviz's adjustment basis is undocumented: check splits, spin-offs and dividends and their effective dates; do not splice incompatible series or assert a point-in-time basis you cannot verify. Timing alone does not establish cause.
2. **Earnings reactions:** Seek the last twelve actual releases within available coverage, or state why fewer are verifiable. For each, verify release date/time and session from the release itself or another dated primary source; 8-K acceptance is filing time and may follow release. Identify the matching pre-release and reaction sessions, base/reaction closes, issuer return, same-window market and sector returns, and confounders. Use `derived/earnings_events.csv` only as a candidate calculation to check, not proof of release timing or a complete list. Handle 6-K/foreign issuers through their actual disclosures. If time is unknown at the cutoff, mark the reaction window UNKNOWN. Estimates/surprises require a dated attributable source.
3. **Short interest:** Give settlement date, verified dissemination date or UNKNOWN, shares short, float and average-volume definitions/dates, computed days-to-cover formula, trend and split/revision caveats. Distinguish FINRA's volume denominator from Finviz's. Reg SHO daily short-sale volume is venue-limited trade flow: report dates, `Market` coverage and `ShortVolume/TotalVolume` denominator; it is neither open short interest nor net bearish positioning. Borrow fees/utilization are UNKNOWN unless timestamped evidence is obtained.
4. **Options:** For an eligible snapshot, state retrieval time, contract last-trade age, underlying price/time, expiry coverage, moneyness definition, comparable IV level/term structure/skew, open-interest and volume concentrations, and missing/wide bid-ask quotes. Define the sample and exclusions for every aggregate. No IV percentile without history; prints and put/call ratios do not identify opening trades, motive or net exposure.
5. **Insiders and holders:** Cite filings-reader claim IDs when available. Otherwise report only transaction/holding observations verified against originals, with dates, class, reporting lags and coverage; distinguish sales, withholding, awards, proposed Form 144 sales and Rule 10b5-1 disclosures. Do not infer motive, sum joint 13D/G reporters or call a missing 13F position a sale. State whether manager 13F, fund holdings and N-PORT were actually reviewed; a Finviz managers export has no ticker filter.
6. **Aggregator snapshot:** If eligible, show Finviz `Target Price`, `Analyst Recom` and estimate fields as unattributed, undated-provider CLAIMs with cache retrieval time, definitions/analyst counts UNKNOWN. A quoted aggregator target is not your target or an observed market expectation. Summarize agreement, conflicts and limits across signals without a trading recommendation.

## Cutoff and finish

For historical `AS_OF`, verify both observation and first public availability for each price, filing, short-interest and Reg SHO item; settlement/event date alone is insufficient. Later adjusted price data cannot prove a past price basis. Live options, news and screener snapshots retrieved after `AS_OF` are ineligible; never backcast them. Save every return/ratio's code, formula and inputs in `work/`; use `POS-` claim IDs and contract labels/dates/units/gap vocabulary. No own target price, buy/sell/hold call or numeric probability.

Finish when parts 1–6 are supported or have precise gaps and likely effects, and `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl`, `search_log.md` exist. Stop when sources are exhausted or origins repeat; use `BLOCKED` for a missing required brief/cache identity, not an optional dataset. Reply in at most 15 lines.
