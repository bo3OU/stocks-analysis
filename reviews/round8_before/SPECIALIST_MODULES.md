# Specialist modules (assigned in `FOCUS` when material)

Each assigned module is recorded in `findings.md` coverage as used / not applicable / relevant but not obtained, with the reason. Do not bury the core analysis under low-relevance data. Source status is in `SOURCE_LIBRARY.md`; rules for flags are in `RULEBOOK_FILINGS.md`.

| Module | Owner | How, and safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| Insider transactions (Forms 3/4/5/144) | filings reader (the one ledger); management and positioning use its output and never recompute | about three years; read transaction codes, footnotes, ownership changes, 10b5-1 disclosure; distinguish purchases and sales from exercises, awards and tax withholding; a Form 144 is a notice, not an executed sale; no motive |
| Institutional and fund holdings (13F, 13D/G, N-PORT) | filings reader for 13D/G; positioning for 13F and fund coverage | dated snapshots; use the latest complete reporting period; map security class and CUSIP; account for splits; avoid double counting managers and funds; top-holder lists are incomplete; a missing position is not a sale; 13F shows no shorts, execution dates, motives or current holdings; verify N-PORT availability and lag. The Finviz managers and funds exports have no ticker filter |
| Short interest and lending | positioning | FINRA files (tested): settlement and publication dates, float and days-to-cover method; Reg SHO daily short volume is trade flow, never short interest; borrow fees and utilization need a timestamped source (none configured: UNKNOWN) |
| Options | positioning | Finviz chain is one snapshot: IV, term structure, skew, open interest, volume, spreads; define moneyness; no IV percentile without history; prints and ratios do not show opening or closing or net exposure |
| Bonds and credit | positioning supplies dated trades and terms; valuation calculates yields | identify bonds from issuer debt documents; TRACE-type trade data access is untested; state trade time, price convention, liquidity, seniority, call terms; match Treasury or credit comparisons by date and duration; distinguish a yield difference from an option-adjusted spread; "no bonds identified" differs from "trade data inaccessible" |
| Government awards | geopolitics | USAspending (autocomplete tested only): match legal entities, subsidiaries and recipient identifiers; distinguish award ceiling, obligations, outlays and company revenue; avoid double counting modifications; no match is not proof of no government business |
| Healthcare and regulatory | geopolitics | FDA approval and clearance records, labels, recalls, trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov tested; openFDA is not a complete regulatory record), published trial evidence; check product, sponsor, indication, jurisdiction and commercial rights; distinguish approval, clearance, authorization, submission and planned milestone; event-report counts do not establish causation or incidence |
| Imports and supply chain | geopolitics | documented shipment data (ImportYeti is blocked; others untested) plus counterparty disclosures; state transport mode, geography, window, entity matching and gaps; shipments are not revenue, production or total procurement |
| Hiring and restructuring | management | careers pages, reliable archives, WARN notices (California workbook tested: rolling window), disclosed restructuring plans; a live ad is not a hire; deduplicate reposts and agency ads; compare history only with a consistent observed method; no inference to revenue or distress |
| Political disclosures | management, only if material to a specific question | official House and Senate reports and amendments (House index reachable, Senate blocked); keep amount ranges, transaction and filing dates, owner attribution and reporting limits; no exact dollars, no inference of privileged information |
