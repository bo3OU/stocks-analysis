## Extract, in priority order
1. **Business:** Identify changes in customers, products, pricing, recurring revenue, contract terms, recognition, segments/geography, concentration, seasonality, backlog, warranties, manufacturing dependencies and product stage.
2. **Obligations and financing:** Report debt terms/maturities, facility conditions, restricted cash, leases, purchase/JV commitments, pensions, contingencies, proceedings, contract liabilities and subsequent events; options/RSUs/PSUs, converts, warrants, preferred, earnouts, ATM/shelf, buybacks and splits. Separate capacity from cash raised, authorization from execution, and dated cover-page/balance-sheet shares from weighted basic/diluted shares. Put dated debt, leases, dilution and corporate actions with claim IDs in `findings.md` for financials and valuation.
3. **Disclosure changes:** Compare annual risk factors, policies/estimates, critical audit matters, auditor, going-concern and control language in context. Compare recurring non-GAAP adjustments with GAAP; leave trends to financials. A removed risk is not proof it ended.
4. **Events:** For domestic issuers, date relevant 8-Ks by accession, event and filing time; use item codes only to triage. Open primary documents and material exhibits, especially 1.01, 2.03, 3.02 and 5.02; separately note 4.01/4.02. The fetcher caps exhibits at six and may omit 3.02/5.02 exhibits: inspect `index.json` before claiming complete coverage. Identify EX-99.1 by content, not as a presumed transcript.
5. **Ownership:** (save the issuer-matched transaction ledger as `work/insider_ledger.csv` with the fields named in the contract) Review roughly three years of issuer-matched Forms 3/4/5, 144 and 13D/G; state dates, caps and legacy unparsed filings. `*_other_issuers.csv` and `ownership_13dg_target_as_filer.csv` concern other issuers. Reopen raw XML for material flags, relationships, footnotes and exemptions. In a saved script with input/output copies, separate non-derivative common-share P/S from grants, exercises, withholding and derivatives. Group by verified person CIK, security, ownership basis and period; adjust for splits/ADR ratios. Avoid double-counting joint reports: if a transaction cannot be assigned to one person, show it once as joint and omit person-level net. Show gross buys/sales and net comparable shares. Parsed `owner_cik` is only the first reporter: check `owner_ciks` and XML. Filing-level `rule_10b5_1` is not row-level proof. Extract plan adoption date; unknown modification, results disclosure, exemption or rule means UNKNOWN. For FPI R10B5-A, use results-bearing 20-F/6-K as specified in the rulebook. Apply R10B5-B Item 408(a) comparisons only where relevant 10-Q/10-K disclosure applies. Apply S16-A/B/C, R10B5-A/B, F144-A and S13-A within verified scope; verify unverified legal text at the SEC. Six-month P/S pairs are candidates, not violations or computed profits. Match Form 144 notices to sales by person, class and date within Form 4 coverage; unmatched notices are questions only where Form 4 reporting applies. Date 13D/G snapshots by person/class; never sum joint reporters or infer current holdings.
6. **Proxy:** If available by AS_OF, give beneficial ownership, related parties, auditor fees and board facts; leave compensation design and management track record to management.
