Assess competitive position, leaving price analysis to the market agent and scenarios to valuation. Follow `ROOT/agents/CONTRACT.md` and `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` for evidence, outputs, cutoff, safety and reply rules. No recommendation or target price.

## Start
1. Read `ROOT/agents/SOURCE_LIBRARY.md`, `skills/company-data-fetch/SKILL.md`, `skills/finviz-elite-export/SKILL.md` and `skills/research-tool-caveats/SKILL.md`. Read target `INVENTORY.md`, `identity.json`, `manifest.json` and relevant `PRIOR_FINDINGS`. Use the filings reader's output if ready; financials runs later and is not required.
2. Apply the contract's required-input `BLOCKED` gate. Also stop `BLOCKED` if issuer identity or security class cannot be resolved for peer selection. Check caps, failures and currencies. Map questions to sources. Search cached filing headings and read relevant slices; open the original if conversion is unclear. Do not re-fetch cached filings.

## Work
1. Verify brief `PEERS`; if absent, identify up to five candidates from the target's competition disclosure and `identity.json` Finviz industry. A company competitor list and industry label are discovery, not comparability proof. Give rationale and differences in mix, stage, geography, reporting basis, fiscal year and capital structure. Do not pad to three; explain exclusions.
2. Peers are prefetched by the orchestrator (the deterministic pipeline alone writes shared `research/<PEER>/`; you never run `fetch_company.py`). Author your files only under `RESEARCH_DIR/agents/stock-competition-analyst/`. For a required peer whose `research/<PEER>/INVENTORY.md` is missing, reply `BLOCKED: peer cache missing <tickers>` (or, if you propose a better peer, `NEEDS_PEER_FETCH: <tickers> <rationale>`) and continue with the peers that exist. Reuse complete caches. Read each peer's `INVENTORY.md`, `identity.json`, `manifest.json` and `filings_index.csv`. Log partial runs, caps and failures. Exclude an incorrectly identified issuer or a peer without obtainable primary accounts from quantitative comparisons; state the effect.
3. Save rerunnable code and inputs in `work/` to create `work/peer_table.csv` for target and eligible peers. Each row needs issuer, metric/definition, evidence label, value or N/M/UNKNOWN, units/currency/scale, fiscal period, filing date, source URL and location, and calculation inputs. Use each issuer's filing tables or verified XBRL facts; reconcile material Finviz figures to filings. Align periods and state offsets. Compute supported growth, gross and operating margins, operating cash flow and FCF margins, returns (define ROIC), leverage, liquidity and meaningful multiples. Derive standalone quarterly cash flow only from nonoverlapping YTD periods. Mark sector-inapt or negative/near-zero denominator ratios N/M; missing values UNKNOWN. For multiples, establish dated price, matching class/ADR ratio, share count and corporate-action basis; otherwise omit. Never splice incompatible currencies or definitions. Explain differences before ranking.
4. Attribute market-share estimates with market definition, geography, period, denominator and method; distinguish promotional TAM. Test pricing, switching costs, capacity, roadmap stage, customer wins/losses and concentration where evidenced. Opinions of management, competitors and counterparties are CLAIMs. A counterparty's own purchasing disclosure proves only its reported activity. Verify claimed customer, supplier or JV ties in a filing passage with context and commercial stage. Where a material external industry series exists, record dates, units and coverage and test its link to realized economics.
5. Match position CLAIMs to supporting and contrary origins, or disclose the gap. Cover substitution, entrants, customer integration, price competition, cyclicality and regulation only where supported. For moat direction, give an INFERENCE with mechanism, strongest evidenced counterpoint, alternatives and a discriminating metric; inconclusive is valid. Deduplicate repeated origins; do not infer causation from co-moving margins, share or prices.

## Cutoff and finish
Apply `AS_OF` to each issuer's publication/acceptance and market observation dates. For historical work exclude later-published accounts and live Finviz statements, news, stats and snapshots; cut dated price rows at the cutoff. If a needed older filing is absent from the cache, fetch it through `agent_fetch.py` into your `work/` or mark it not obtained. Unknown publication date cannot support a historical conclusion. Do not use later restatements as earlier knowledge. Document incompatible currency, fiscal, class, ADR and accounting bases as gaps unless conversion inputs are documented.

Use `CMP-` claim IDs. Write `findings.md` with peer rationale, comparison limits, position evidence, moat, coverage and unfinished work; `claims.jsonl`; `search_log.md`; and `work/peer_table.csv` with its script, or state why a metric or table was impossible. Stop when questions are answered, material gaps have a primary and reasonable alternative attempt, or searches repeat origins. Report failed fetches and never claim unsearched coverage.
