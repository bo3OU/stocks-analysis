## Extract, in priority order
1. **Business:** Latest annual and interim disclosures: customers and products, pricing where stated, recurring revenue, contract terms, recognition, segments and geography, concentration, seasonality, backlog definition, warranties, manufacturing dependencies and new-product stage. Use the last three annuals/eight interims where available to identify material changes. Attribute forecasts, plans, product-readiness and management assertions as CLAIM, not FACT.
2. **Obligations and financing:** Debt terms and maturity schedule, facility conditions, restricted cash, leases, purchase and joint-venture commitments, pensions, contingencies, legal proceedings, contract liabilities, subsequent events; options/RSUs/PSUs, converts, warrants, preferred, earnouts, ATM/shelf, buybacks and splits. Distinguish capacity from cash raised; authorization from execution; cover-page shares (with date), balance-sheet shares, and weighted basic/diluted shares. Give debt, leases, dilutive instruments, share counts and corporate actions prominently in `findings.md` for the financials and valuation agents.
3. **Disclosure changes:** Compare annual risk factors, policy and estimate changes, critical audit matters, auditor, going-concern and control language in context. Compare recurring non-GAAP adjustments in opened releases with GAAP; leave numerical trend analysis to financials. A removed risk is not proof it ended.
4. **Events:** For domestic issuers, date relevant 8-Ks by accession, event date and filing time; use item codes only to triage. Open the primary document and material exhibits, especially 1.01, 2.03, 3.02 and 5.02; give each 4.01/4.02 a separate entry. The fetcher may cap exhibits at six and may omit those for 3.02/5.02: inspect `index.json` before claiming complete coverage. For foreign issuers, use relevant 6-K content. An EX-99.1 is identified by its content, not assumed to be a transcript.
5. **Ownership:** Use issuer-matched derived rows; `*_other_issuers.csv` and `ownership_13dg_target_as_filer.csv` concern other issuers. Reopen raw XML for material flags, relationships, footnotes and exemptions. In a saved script with input/output copies, separate P/S non-derivative common-share trades from grants, exercises, withholding and derivatives; group by person CIK, security and period. Show gross buys/sales and net comparable shares. Filing-level `rule_10b5_1` does not establish which row is covered. Extract the relevant adoption date; unknown modification date, reporting-quarter date, exemption or rule means UNKNOWN, not a breach. Apply S16-A/B/C, R10B5-A/B, F144-A and S13-A only within verified scope; verify unverified legal text at its primary SEC source before stating it as fact. Six-month P/S pairs are candidates, never violations or computed profits. Match Form 144 notices to sales by person, class and date within searched coverage; a missing match is a review question. Report 13D/G snapshots by person and class; do not sum joint reporters or infer current holdings. State caps and legacy unparsed filings.
6. **Proxy:** If available by AS_OF, give beneficial ownership, related parties, auditor fees and board facts; leave compensation design and management track record to management.
