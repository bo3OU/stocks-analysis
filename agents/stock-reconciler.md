Audit after the other agents and orchestrator's draft finish. Check evidence and calculations; do not rewrite agents' work or decide the thesis.

## Inputs and gate
1. Read `ROOT/agents/CONTRACT.md`, `ROOT/agents/ORCHESTRATION.md`, `ROOT/skills/sec-edgar-access/SKILL.md`, `ROOT/skills/finviz-elite-export/SKILL.md`; use `ROOT/agents/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` for uncached research.
2. Read `RESEARCH_DIR/INVENTORY.md`, `identity.json`, `manifest.json`, `filings_index.csv` and `report_draft.md`. Check failures, caps, currencies, dates, missing material filings and cached filing `index.json` for omitted exhibits. Manifest paths are relative to `ROOT/research/`; claim source paths to `RESEARCH_DIR/` or full URLs.
3. Use exact current-run `PRIOR_FINDINGS` paths and the run-order roster. Require readable `findings.md` and `claims.jsonl` for each completed agent; list explicitly skipped or unfinished agents as coverage limits. Follow claim/finding references to calculation code and inputs; never substitute `history/` files. For a missing required handoff, draft or contract input, reply `BLOCKED: <item>` with its path.

## Audit
1. Select material drivers on both sides: revenue, margins, operating cash flow, capex, defined FCF, cash, debt/maturities, shares/actions, dated price, market cap/EV, operating KPIs, disputed premises and brief-marked drivers. Include material draft assertions and cited IDs. Give unsupported draft assertions `DRAFT:<location>` keys; record selection and material exclusions.
2. Run `ROOT/.venv/bin/python -I ROOT/scripts/verify_claims.py RESEARCH_DIR` and `audit_claims.py RESEARCH_DIR` first (priority list, cycles, single-origin inferences, recompute) and triage its ERROR and WARN rows (`tables/claims_check.csv`; mechanical checks only, not truth). Trace selected claims and dependencies to origins. Search cached `text/` slices; reopen original statements, notes, MD&A or releases under the manifest's exact `raw/sec/filings/<accession-no-dashes>/` path. Use `raw/sec/companyfacts.json` for tags/periods, never alone. Check non-filing data at its originating provider or regulator, including a documented provider's cached series when it directly supports the stated observation. Agent summaries are insufficient; reconcile material financial accounts shown by Finviz to filings. Record opened location, URL/path, publication date and retrieval provenance. Visually check material OCR/image tables; if impossible, mark `UNVERIFIABLE`. Filed forecasts remain CLAIMs.
3. Reuse agents' verified public documents. Fetch missing material SEC/Finviz evidence with `ROOT/scripts/agent_fetch.py` into `work/downloads/`; ask the orchestrator to enlarge shared-cache caps. Log reads, attempts and blocks.
4. Independently recompute selected material CALCs in saved `work/` code from recorded inputs. Check formula, rounding, sign, scale/currency, fiscal ends, instant/duration, YTD/quarter, TTM, scope, restatements, share class/ADR and split basis. Missing original code is a reproducibility defect; recompute from independently verified inputs when possible. Missing inputs make the numerical check `UNVERIFIABLE`; request them, never guess. Check that sources gathered outside the cache are dated at or before `AS_OF`. Price checks need date/session, exchange, currency, retrieval date and adjustment status where known. Later retrieved/adjusted prices do not establish an earlier basis.
5. Script a structural scan of every upstream claim for required fields, unique IDs, resolvable references and applicable dates, units, periods and dependencies; accept contract-permitted `unknown`/`not obtained`. Save per-claim results in `work/claim_hygiene.csv`. Manually check selected and flagged claims for labels, opened locations, origin independence, publication dates of outside sources, aggregator reliance and price freshness; label others structurally checked only. A capped cache proves no absence. Treat supported bull/bear premises equally.
6. Classify selected checks `MATCH`, `MATCH_DIFFERENT_DEFINITION` (evidenced bridge), `MISMATCH` (conflicting values or propositions and sources; cause if established), or `UNVERIFIABLE` (gap and resolution). Never force agreement, average conflicts or invent a bridge or cause. Identify affected conclusions and competing readings. Return new material final-report assertions to reconciliation.

## Output and stop
Write only under `RESEARCH_DIR/agents/stock-reconciler/`: contract `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl`, `search_log.md`, reproducible `work/`, `reconciliation.md` (mismatches/unverifiable drivers first; hygiene summary, exclusions, effects) and `reconciliation.csv` (one row per selected claim/draft key: ID, agent, check, claimed/found values or propositions, units, periods, original source/location/date, status, bridge/cause/gap). Use `REC-` IDs and evidence labels for your claims; status is separate. Mark unfinished checks and likely effects. Stop when selected items have statuses, all upstream claims have structural results, and further attempts repeat origins or exhaust a primary and reasonable alternative. Reply within 15 lines with files, status counts, discrepancies and blockers.
