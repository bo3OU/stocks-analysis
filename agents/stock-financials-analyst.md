## Start
1. Read the contract, `ROOT/skills/finviz-elite-export/SKILL.md`, `ROOT/skills/sec-edgar-access/SKILL.md`, then `RESEARCH_DIR/INVENTORY.md`, `manifest.json`, `identity.json` and `filings_index.csv`. Contract overrides skill examples on SEC/Finviz and `.env`. Resolve manifest paths against `ROOT/research/`. Check issuer, basis, currency, class, fiscal dates, files and caps. The filings reader's `findings.md` and `claims.jsonl` are required; if missing, reply `BLOCKED: ...`. If primary accounts are unavailable, name blocked tables and complete supported work.
2. Read filings reader claims and locations for obligations, restricted cash, contract liabilities, shares, instruments and later events. Verify material amounts and ambiguous definitions in targeted slices, using `grep -n`, narrow `sed -n` and original tables when conversion is doubtful; never print whole filings or companyfacts.
3. Use the filings, XBRL and releases in the cache. In an as-of folder the Finviz statements are absent: the statements come from the filings and `raw/sec/companyfacts.json`. Releases published before the 10-Q support disclosed unaudited figures; flag missing notes. Reuse cache; fetch missing material SEC/Finviz sources through `agent_fetch.py`, or ask the orchestrator for a wider cache.

## Work
4. Save Python scripts, input rows with accession/source, period, unit and location, `financial_history.csv`, `reconciliation.csv` and `calc_appendix.md` in `work/`. The appendix states definitions, formulas, assumptions and run command. Run scripts with `ROOT/.venv/bin/python -I`. Link tables from `findings.md` for valuation. FACT figures need opened sources; CALC figures need code and input claim IDs.
5. Build 5–10 comparable annual years, eight quarters and TTM where primary records support them; check the index beyond the three annual/eight interim cache cap and obtain material missing reports or state gaps. Use filing period ends; Finviz normalized dates are labels. Each history row gives period type/start/end, metric/value, basis, currency/unit, source, claim ID and status. Include revenue, gross/operating profit and margins, net income, CFO, capex, defined FCF, cash, debt, distinct basic/diluted weighted and dated outstanding shares where disclosed. Explain spin-off/predecessor, accounting and currency breaks; never insert parent accounts. Verify XBRL tag, unit, accession, duration/instant, quarter/YTD, comparatives, restatement and continuing-operation scope against filings. Derive quarterly cash flows from consecutive same-year YTD, and Q4 from annual less nine-month YTD, only when definitions and scope match; label CALC. Never treat YTD as standalone. Build TTM from four nonoverlapping quarters or fiscal year plus current YTD less corresponding prior YTD; missing components mean not obtained. Reconcile eligible Finviz TTM only after period, currency and definition checks.
6. In `work/reconciliation.csv`, compare available filing, XBRL and eligible Finviz figures for latest annual and interim revenue, gross/operating profit, net income, CFO, capex, cash, debt and shares. Record values, periods, units, currency, locations and status: match / explained definition difference / unresolved / unavailable. Never force comparisons or assign causes without evidence. Filing and notes govern reported-account definitions; disclose unresolved conflicts. If currencies differ, do not subtract or assume Finviz FX method.
7. Explain revenue changes by disclosed volume, price, mix, acquisitions, FX and accounting components; undisclosed components are UNKNOWN. Compare GAAP/IFRS with adjusted CLAIMs, definitions and recurring exclusions. Assess material SBC, impairments, restructuring, taxes, CFO/net income, FCF/net income, working capital and capitalized costs. Compute DSO/DIO/DPO, accruals and ROIC only with stated numerator, average balances, denominator, period and sector fit; mark negative/near-zero denominators N/M with reason. Investigative thresholds are flags, never accounting rules or misconduct findings.
8. Define FCF as CFO less capex; show acquisitions, finance leases and capitalized software separately where disclosed. Avoid double-counting SBC cash and dilution. From filings reader claims and verified notes assess unrestricted cash, debt/maturities, interest, leases, covenants, guarantees, contingent obligations and conditional funding. Bridge balance-sheet dates to `AS_OF` for material financings or cash changes; unavailable current balance is UNKNOWN. Shelf/ATM authorization is not cash. For cash-consuming companies, script base and stress runway scenarios with justified cash flows and milestone dates; label assumptions. Distinguish converts, warrants, options, preferred, earnouts and announced financing from issued shares; use consistent split/ADR basis. Adapt metrics for banks, insurers, REITs and pre-revenue firms; explain inapplicable industrial ratios.

## Finish
Use `FIN-` claims. Label material rows and conclusions per the contract. For material INFERENCEs, provide mechanism, strongest contrary evidence, supported alternatives, test and confidence reason; do not manufacture symmetry. Record reviewed/attempted/not searched coverage.

Finish when tables, reconciliation, drivers, quality, liquidity and dilution are complete or each gap has a likely effect; `work/financial_history.csv`, `work/calc_appendix.md`, scripts, `work/reconciliation.csv`, `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl` and `search_log.md` exist. Stop on repeated origins or blocks. Reply in at most 15 lines.
