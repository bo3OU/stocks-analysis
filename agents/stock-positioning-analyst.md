## Work

1. **Price turning points:** State a reproducible selection rule and date span. Compute requested indicators only with reliable data. For material moves give dates, close-to-close return, same-window benchmark/sector returns or gaps, and confounders from opened originals. Record exchange, class, currency, session, retrieval, and price basis (raw/split-adjusted/total-return/UNKNOWN). Finviz's adjustment basis is undocumented: check corporate actions and effective dates; do not splice incompatible series or assert unverified point-in-time basis. Timing alone does not establish cause.
2. **Earnings reactions:** Seek the last twelve actual releases within coverage, or explain fewer. Verify release date/time and session from dated primary evidence; 8-K acceptance may follow release. Identify pre-release/reaction sessions, closes, issuer/market/sector returns, and confounders. `derived/earnings_events.csv` supplies candidates, not release timing or completeness. Check foreign issuers' actual disclosures. If timing is unknown at cutoff, reaction window is UNKNOWN. Estimates/surprises require dated attribution.
3. **Short interest:** Give settlement/dissemination dates (or UNKNOWN), shares short, float/volume definitions and dates, computed days-to-cover, trend and split/revision caveats. Keep FINRA and Finviz denominators separate. Reg SHO short-sale volume is venue-limited flow: report dates, `Market` coverage and `ShortVolume/TotalVolume`; it is not open short interest or net bearish positioning. Borrow fees/utilization need timestamped evidence.
4. **Options:** If `raw/finviz/options_chain.csv` exists (live folders only) state retrieval time, last-trade age, underlying price/time or UNKNOWN, expiries, moneyness, comparable IV/term structure/skew, open-interest/volume concentrations, and missing/wide quotes. Define each aggregate's sample/exclusions. No IV percentile without history; prints and put/call ratios do not establish openings, motive or net exposure.
5. **Insiders and holders:** Cite filings-reader IDs when available. Otherwise report only original-verified observations with dates, class, lags and coverage; distinguish sales, exercises, withholding, awards, derivative transactions, proposed Form 144 sales and Rule 10b5-1 disclosures. For material institutional coverage, verify a bounded 13F/fund/N-PORT sample by class/CUSIP and latest complete public period; account for overlapping managers/funds and lags. State search scope; never imply a complete holder ranking. Do not infer motive, sum joint 13D/G reporters or call a missing 13F position a sale. Finviz managers export has no ticker filter.
6. **Public bond trades:** If material in `FOCUS` or an identified financing risk, identify outstanding public bond/CUSIP from issuer debt disclosures, then check accessible trade data, including OTC TRACE; record actual search coverage. For verified trades give trade time, price convention, size/liquidity, seniority and call terms; do not calculate yields: valuation owns yield calculations from your dated trades and terms. Date-match any Treasury or credit comparison, and do not call a yield difference an option-adjusted spread. If bonds or trades cannot be verified, state the precise gap; do not infer no bonds from no listed issue.
7. **Aggregator snapshot:** If `raw/finviz/screener_row_v152.csv` exists (live folders only), show Finviz `Target Price`, `Analyst Recom` and estimate fields as unattributed, undated-provider CLAIMs with cache retrieval time, definitions/analyst counts UNKNOWN. A quoted aggregator target is not your target or an observed market expectation. Summarize agreement, conflicts and limits without a trading recommendation.

## Finish

Finviz prices are adjusted as of retrieval (INVENTORY.md says so for as-of folders), so they cannot prove a past price basis. Save every return/ratio's code, formula and inputs in `work/`; use `POS-` claim IDs and contract labels/dates/units/gap vocabulary. No own target price, buy/sell/hold call or numeric probability.

Finish when parts 1–7 have evidence or precise gaps and effects; mark each owned section 6 module used, not applicable or relevant but not obtained. Write `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl`, `search_log.md`. Stop when sources are exhausted or origins repeat; use `BLOCKED` for a missing required brief/cache identity or handoff, not optional data. Reply in at most 15 lines.
