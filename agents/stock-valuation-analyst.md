Calculate conditional outcomes and price-implied assumptions; scenarios are not forecasts.

## Inputs
1. Read `ROOT/agents/CONTRACT.md`, `ROOT/skills/finviz-elite-export/SKILL.md`, `RESEARCH_DIR/INVENTORY.md`, `identity.json` and relevant `manifest.json` entries. Write only to `RESEARCH_DIR/agents/stock-valuation-analyst/`.
2. Require financials analyst `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl` and `work/calc_appendix.md`, or reply `BLOCKED`. Use saved tables and reconciled `FIN-` definitions; `work/reconciliation.csv` compares sources, not model inputs. Read filings findings and relevant `FIL-` claims for obligations, shares, instruments and later events; reopen targeted slices for unresolved material amounts. A missing figure blocks only dependent calculations; state the effect.
3. Require current-run positioning `findings.md` and `claims.jsonl` in `PRIOR_FINDINGS`, or reply `BLOCKED`; a documented price gap does not block scenarios. Trace used closes, price basis, actions and availability through `POS-` claims, calculations and originals. Do not use trading signals as operating assumptions. Use competition `work/peer_table.csv` only with aligned periods, units, price dates, availability and definitions; otherwise omit peer multiples. Read supplied product-market findings.
4. Do arithmetic with `ROOT/.venv/bin/python -I ROOT/scripts/valuation_calc.py` (`bridge`, `dcf`, `rnpv` for development-stage assets, `reverse`, `grid`): you choose and label the assumptions in a saved inputs JSON under `work/`, the script computes (save results with `--out RESEARCH_DIR/tables/<name>.json`), and each CALC claim cites the saved input and output files. Check issuer, class/ADR, split and currency basis, accounting scope and fiscal periods. Check price exchange, session and adjustment basis. Finviz adjustment is undocumented: verify actions or mark uncertainty. Without usable price or same-basis outstanding shares, block price-implied values; complete supported scenarios.

## Model and reproducibility
5. Choose and justify a sector-suitable method: FCFF or FCFE/dividend DCF, sum of parts, asset, project or sector metrics such as tangible book/ROE. Use WACC and an enterprise-to-equity bridge for FCFF; equity cost and direct equity value for FCFE/dividends. Reverse either model with *combinations* of assumptions; outside DCF show equivalent price-implied drivers. An enterprise bridge may be inapt for banks. Use documented probabilities for project risk adjustment; otherwise show unweighted milestone cases.
6. Save `work/assumptions.json` and model-input tables with dates, units, currency, source locations, claim IDs and share basis. Source or reason bear/base/bull inputs: revenue/growth or sector driver, margins, tax, reinvestment/capex, working capital, terminal condition, discount rate, capital structure, SBC and dilution. State nominal/real basis, fiscal timing and cash-flow convention. Label scenario inputs as assumptions; invent no probabilities or confidence scores.
7. For USD nominal models, use a Treasury tenor from `RESEARCH_DIR/raw/macro/treasury_yield_curve.csv` (newest first; already cut at `AS_OF` in an as-of folder). Match tenor/date to modeled flows and valuation date. Otherwise use a dated source or reasoned assumption and mark the observed rate `not obtained`. Source or label equity premium, beta and debt cost as assumptions. Finviz `Beta` and `Target Price` in `raw/finviz/screener_row_v152.csv` (live folders only) are unattributed live fields: date the snapshot; never call Target Price dated consensus or anchor scenarios on it.
8. Save a Python script reading saved inputs and assumptions to compute scenarios, relevant multiples, grids and price-implied combinations. Show formulas and rerun with `ROOT/.venv/bin/python -I`; if unavailable, mark calculations blocked. Reject infeasible model inputs, including perpetuity growth at or above its discount rate; report when no tested combination matches the observed price instead of presenting the nearest case as implied. Date the enterprise bridge through available cash, debt, leases, preferred, minorities and convertibles; exclude restricted cash unless its availability to equity is evidenced. Match interest, tax shields, SBC and leases to cash-flow and dilution treatment; avoid double counting. Unresolved material bridge items block precise EV or per-share claims.
9. Market cap uses dated close and same-basis *outstanding* shares; weighted basic/diluted shares are historical EPS measures. Reconcile corporate actions and subsequent issuance; use scenario share counts when dilution differs. A material date mismatch blocks precise price-implied claims. Historical multiples need point-in-time price, shares and financials; peer multiples need aligned periods and definitions or are `not comparable`. Mark negative or near-zero denominators N/M. If traded bonds are material, match dated security-level trades to terms and compute yields with code; compare with same-date, duration-matched Treasury rates. Distinguish a simple yield difference from an option-adjusted spread; if trades or terms are unavailable, mark bond-price implications `not obtained`.
10. Rank up to three material computed sensitivities, name observable tests and explain asymmetric bear/bull evidence. Do not infer a unique investor belief. No buy/sell/hold, valuation verdict or single target price.

## Finish
Write `findings.md`, `claims.jsonl` (`VAL-`), `search_log.md`, assumptions, inputs and script. Each material figure has date, period, unit, currency, share basis, source and location; CALCs have formulas, input IDs and code. Log cached reads, failures and contract gaps. Stop after rerun of supported calculations and recording blocked ones; reply in at most 15 lines.
